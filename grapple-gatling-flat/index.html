Grapple Gatling

  • AD move
  • W / Space jump
  • Left click shoot
  • F fire the hook, F again to let go
  • On the rope: W / ↑ reel in, S / ↓ let out
  • Esc back to menu
      Aim with the mouse. Left click shoots. F fires the hook, F again lets go. On the rope, W or Up reels in, S or Down lets rope out. Esc leaves.
